Club Leader

In 2017, I joined Alexandria Treetop Toastmasters in Alexandria, Virginia, and served as Vice President for Public Relations. Within a year, members elected me president. I introduced a new strategy to strengthen the club:

  • Financial incentives to encourage long-term membership and commitment.
  • Established an online payment system for membership dues.
  • Introduced a friendship program to strengthen relationships and mentorship among members.
  • Mentored a new team of officers throughout the year before handing over leadership at the end of my term.

These initiatives transformed a small underdog club into one of the most successful and competitive clubs in District 27. The district leadership, representing 150 clubs, recognized our turnaround as its top success story of 2019.

Public Speaking Champion

I was the first officer in the club to adopt a new online public speaking curriculum, earning the club’s first Triple Crown award in less than a year.

I delivered more than 100 speeches to audiences ranging from 10 to 500 people and received valuable feedback from public speaking professionals.

As I developed my skills, I started winning speech contests and earned several public speaking awards, including:

  • Best Speech in Arlington, Virginia, 2018, after winning two qualifying rounds.
  • Second place for Best Speech in the Southern DC Division, 2018, after winning three qualifying rounds.
  • Best Speech Evaluator in Alexandria, Virginia, 2018, for providing feedback to another public speaker, after winning two qualifying rounds.
