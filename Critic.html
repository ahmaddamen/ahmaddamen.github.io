Expertise

Film Acquisition & Criticism

From discovering documentaries at international film markets to bringing them to television audiences, I approach film as both a buyer and a filmmaker.

Selected licensing partners

Broadcasters, distributors and production companies

Film Buyer

  • Licensed 600+ hours of documentaries and built a network of 500+ licensors worldwide as the main Film Buyer for Voice of America.
  • Scouted films at Cannes, MipDoc, IDFA and Realscreen, building relationships with international distributors and production houses.
  • Selected films for their originality, storytelling and cultural significance.
  • Acquired and commissioned documentaries in response to some of the most culturally significant events of our time, from #MeToo and Black Lives Matter to COVID-19, Thailand’s cave rescue and the Notre-Dame fire.

Contract Negotiations

  • Commissioned and acquired documentaries from international production houses and reporters at VOA and Al Jazeera English.
  • Negotiated documentary licensing deals with major broadcasters, independent distributors and filmmakers.
  • Balanced broadcast needs with the creative vision of filmmakers, including content adaptation for multiple languages.

Film Critic
